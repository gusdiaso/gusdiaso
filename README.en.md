## About

I'm a data engineer who builds the foundation that lets a business trust its own data: orchestrated pipelines, tested models and platforms that a team can operate and evolve. Today I support and evolve production pipelines in an environment built on Databricks, dbt, Airflow and Snowflake.

## What I do

- **Data pipelines:** ingestion, transformation and orchestration (ETL/ELT) in a lakehouse architecture, with bronze, silver and gold layers.
- **Modeling and quality:** dimensional modeling and data quality tests in dbt, so data reaches consumers already validated.
- **Support and reliability:** failure diagnosis, model fixes, access management and incident lifecycle automation.
- **CI/CD and infrastructure as code:** automated deployment, tests on every change and separate development and production environments.
- **AI applied to data:** machine learning, time series, RAG and the use of LLMs in internal engineering tools.

## My vision

The value of data is not only in analysis, but in the ability to structure, process and deliver it efficiently and reliably. That is why I treat quality, testing and monitoring as part of the delivery, not as a later step.

I believe the path leads to increasingly robust data architectures and to the integration of data engineering with artificial intelligence, so that every solution has a direct impact on the business.

## How I work

- **I start with the business problem.** I gather requirements from the people who will use the solution and only then choose the technology, rather than learning a tool for its own sake.
- **I plan just enough to start.** I break complex problems into smaller tasks and detail only what is needed to move forward.
- **I deliver with tests and automation.** Version control, review, automated tests and continuous deployment are part of my daily work.
- **I document and communicate clearly,** in Portuguese and English.
- **I use AI as a productivity tool, with review.** I work with Claude and Claude Code to understand problems and to generate code and documentation, and I validate the logic and security of what is generated before applying it.

## What I enjoy working with

- Modern data platforms and lakehouse architecture.
- Orchestration and data quality, where engineering protects the business the most.
- Automating data operations, including the use of LLMs to speed up diagnosis and support.
- Bridging data engineering and artificial intelligence.
